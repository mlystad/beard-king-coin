Privacy notice

Last updated 24 November 2026

This page explains what originalbeardking.com collects before the coins go on sale. It is short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you enter it in the pledge form. Nothing else is asked for.

There are no accounts and no payments on this site. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Conceding a coin online

The “send it” form builds a link that carries the names and place you type, in the link itself. Nothing you type there is sent to us or stored on a server. Anyone you share the link with can read what is in it. Your tally of coins conceded is kept only in your own browser and stays there until you clear your site data.

How your email is stored

The pledge form sends your address to a Google Form, so it is stored by Google in a spreadsheet only we can open. Google's own privacy policy applies to that storage.

What we use it for

Only to tell you when the coins are struck and go on sale. We do not sell, rent or share your address.

Services this page relies on

Like any website, the company that hosts this site receives standard request information (such as your IP address and browser) when you visit. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which also receives that information.

Removing your address

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. To have your address deleted entirely, contact us. A contact address will be published here before the first email goes out.

Changes

When coins go on sale, this notice will be updated to cover orders, and the date above will change.
